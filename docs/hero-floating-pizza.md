## Prompt de génération

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: transparent cutout hero image for FORNO., premium artisan pizza truck website. Generate ONLY the food composition, no website UI, text or logo. A photorealistic artisan Neapolitan pizza dramatically separated into several floating triangular slices, staggered vertically in an elegant diagonal composition, one lower cluster and two or three lifted slices above it. Long delicate melted mozzarella strings stretch naturally between slices. Golden blistered crust, rich tomato sauce, creamy mozzarella, a few fresh basil leaves and tiny tomato pieces suspended around the pizza. Inspired by high-end editorial food advertising and the floating exploded pizza hero aesthetic of the TONDO reference. Camera at a three-quarter slightly overhead angle so toppings and crust are appetizing and real. Warm soft studio light from upper left, natural subtle self-shadows on the food, crisp realistic food texture. Isolated on a genuinely transparent alpha background, including the spaces between slices and cheese strings; no opaque backdrop, no white or cream rectangle, no checkerboard baked into pixels. Keep the entire composition and every floating ingredient within frame with comfortable transparent margins. Portrait-ish 4:5 composition designed to fill the right half of a website hero, not too wide, no typography, no plate, no board, no table, no hands, no utensils, no graphic badges, no heavy fake drop shadow, no motion blur. Premium, credible food photograph, not illustration or 3D plastic.
